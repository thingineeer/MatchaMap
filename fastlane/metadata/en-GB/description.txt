MatchaMap is the world map for matcha lovers — a discover, log, and collect app for every matcha you taste, from Tokyo and Kyoto to Seoul, New York, London, and Paris.

[ Features ]
• Global café map — Google Maps powered, with smooth city zoom, custom matcha pins, and clustering.
• Café details — menu, prices, hours, popular times, directions, and Street View on one screen.
• Matcha journal — collect every matcha you taste as a card. Track grade, origin, colour, and tasting notes.
• Ratings and reviews — quick photo reviews and tags for each café.
• Friends feed — see what your friends are drinking on a clean timeline.
• Wishlist — save places you want to visit, grouped by country with a mini world map.
• Sign in with Apple or Passkey — no passwords, fully secure.

[ Why MatchaMap ]
• Colour grading on a 5-step scale — from Pale Spring to Deep Forest. Compare matcha by colour, not just brand.
• 9 origin categories — Uji, Shizuoka, Kagoshima, Korea, Taiwan, and more. Filter cafés by where the leaves come from.
• Mini world map wishlist — turn your travel list into a constellation of stars across the globe.

[ Languages ]
Korean, English (US and UK), Japanese, German, and French — six full localisations. Travel in your language.

[ Get started ]
Open the app, accept location, sign in with Apple or Passkey in one second, and the matcha cafés around you appear on the map.

MatchaMap launches first in Korea, then Japan, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, and France. We do not show ads in the first 60 seconds — you discover first, ads later.
