MatchaMap is the world map for matcha lovers — discover, log, and collect.

- Global map: Powered by Google Maps. City zoom and pins to scan a neighbourhood at a glance.
- Place details: menu, popular times, directions, Street View.
- Matcha journal: collect every matcha you taste. Track grade, origin, colour, and notes.
- Friends feed: see your friends' journals and reviews on a timeline.
- Wishlist: places you want to visit, grouped by country with a mini world map.
- Sign in with Apple / Passkey: no passwords.

Launching first in Korea, Japan, United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and France.
