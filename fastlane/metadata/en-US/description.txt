MatchaMap is the world map for matcha lovers — a discover, log, and collect app for every matcha you taste, from Tokyo and Kyoto to Seoul, New York, London, and Paris.

[ Features ]
• Global cafe map — Google Maps powered, with smooth city zoom, custom matcha pins, and clustering.
• Cafe details — menu, prices, hours, popular times, directions, and Street View on one screen.
• Matcha journal — collect every matcha you taste as a card. Track grade, origin, color, and tasting notes.
• Ratings & reviews — quick photo reviews and tags for each cafe.
• Friends feed — see what your friends are drinking on a clean timeline.
• Wishlist — save places you want to visit, grouped by country with a mini world map.
• Sign in with Apple / Passkey — no passwords, fully secure.

[ Why MatchaMap ]
• Color grading on a 5-step scale — from Pale Spring to Deep Forest. Compare matcha by color, not just brand.
• 9 origin categories — Uji, Shizuoka, Kagoshima, Korea, Taiwan, and more. Filter cafes by where the leaves come from.
• Mini world map wishlist — turn your travel list into a constellation of stars across the globe.

[ Languages ]
Korean, English (US / UK), Japanese, German, and French — six full localizations. Travel in your language.

[ Get started ]
Open the app, accept location, sign in with Apple or Passkey in one second, and the matcha cafes around you appear on the map.

MatchaMap launches first in Korea, then Japan, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, and France. We do not show ads in the first 60 seconds — you discover first, ads later.
